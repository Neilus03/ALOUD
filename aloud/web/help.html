Getting started

ALOUD reads documents aloud and highlights each word as it's spoken, in the text and on the original PDF. Everything runs on your Mac: no account, no upload, no internet needed (except to fetch web links).

  • Open a file: ⌘O, the Open… button, or drop a file on the window.
  • Paste text or a link: ⌘V anywhere in the window (or ⌘⇧V for the paste box).
  • Play: Space. Click any word, in the text or on the PDF, to read from there.

Reads PDF (including scanned PDFs, recognised on your Mac), Word (.docx, .doc), EPUB, MOBI, RTF, OpenDocument, HTML, Markdown, plain text, and web pages.

Text, PDF and both

The buttons at the top (or V) switch between the clean Text view, the original PDF, and Both side by side. The highlight follows the voice in each. In the PDF, parts that are not read (page numbers, footnotes, references…) are shaded when “Show what isn't read” is on.

The sidebar lists the document's sections; click one to jump there. Your place is remembered for every document.

Live figure previews

When ALOUD reads a reference like “Figure 4b” in a PDF, it shows the figure next to the document. It outlines the referenced panel or its label when it can locate it. Click the image to enlarge it.

Choose Read caption to hear the caption and then pick up at the same word. Back to reading returns early. Press Esc to reduce the enlarged figure or dismiss the preview. You can turn off Live figure previews in reading settings.

What ALOUD skips

ALOUD reads the text you'd want to hear and leaves out what gets in the way:

  • page numbers, running headers and footers, line numbers of review copies;
  • footnotes, figure labels, tables, and the reference list, acknowledgements and similar back matter;
  • citations like “[12]” or “(Smith et al., 2020)” (turn off with Skip citations in settings);
  • web clutter: menus, ads, cookie banners, “related posts”, comments.

It expands what should be said differently: “e.g.” becomes “for example”, “$4.2M” “4.2 million dollars”, “Fig. 3” “Figure 3”, and so on. Turn on Show what isn't read to see the skipped parts greyed out.

If a word is pronounced wrongly, right-click it and choose a pronunciation; it's remembered in Pronunciations in settings.

Equations and formulas

Math is read the way a person would say it (“the expected value of Y given X”), not as symbols:

  • LaTeX in pasted text or Markdown (\( … \), $$ … $$, $x^2$), as copied from ChatGPT or Claude;
  • formulas on web pages (Wikipedia, KaTeX, MathJax);
  • formulas inside sentences in PDFs, rebuilt from the page;
  • displayed equations in PDFs, recognised from the page image. They are read in the background, ahead of where you're listening, and appear typeset in the Text view once read.

A formula ALOUD can't rebuild reliably stays silent rather than being read wrongly.

Citations: what is “[26]”?

Press C while listening, or click or right-click a citation such as “[26]” in the text or on the PDF. A card shows the paper's title, first author and year, with links:

  • Open: alphaXiv for arXiv papers, otherwise the DOI page;
  • Google Scholar: a search for the title.

Works with numbered citations (“[3, 7]”), labels (“[BJP12]”) and author–year citations (“Bernheim and Whinston (1986)”). Links open in your normal browser.

Notes while listening

  • M marks the sentence being read. The paragraph gets a yellow edge.
  • N pauses and opens a note box with the sentence quoted. Type, or dictate: press the 🎤 key or Fn twice. Enter saves and resumes reading; ⇧Enter adds a line; Esc cancels.

Your marks and notes are listed under Notes in the sidebar; click one to jump there, × to delete it. They are also saved as Markdown in Documents/ALOUD Notes/<title>.md, grouped by section. Each entry links back to its exact spot: clicking “page 3” in the file opens ALOUD there (while ALOUD is running).

Up next (queue)

In the library, hover a document and press + Up next. When the current document ends, ALOUD says what's next and continues with it, each from where you left off. Press Q or the Up next button in the player to reorder (↑ ↓), remove (×) or clear the queue. Opening a queued document takes it off the queue.

Read anything on your Mac

Select text in any app (Mail, Safari, Notes, a PDF in Preview…) and press ⌃⌥R, or right-click › Services › Read with ALOUD. ALOUD opens it and starts reading, launching itself if needed.

The menu-bar player (book icon) shows what's playing and has Play/Pause, previous/next sentence, Read clipboard and Show ALOUD.

If the shortcut does nothing: System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts › Services › Text › make sure “Read with ALOUD” is ticked. A newly added service sometimes appears only after logging out and in.

Web articles and paywalls

Paste a link (⌘V) and ALOUD opens the page itself, as the site made it, with its pictures, GIFs and videos, and highlights the voice right on it. The switch at the top says Page instead of PDF; Text gives a clean reading version with the pictures inline. Clicking text on the page reads from there; links open in your browser. Menus, ads, comments and “related posts” are not read.

Send to ALOUD from your browser: in settings, drag the blue ⇪ Send to ALOUD button to your bookmarks bar. On any article, click it: the page is sent as you see it, so subscriber articles you're signed in to are read in full. Chrome may ask once to let the site reach your local network; allow it.

Subscriber sites: if a pasted link only gives a preview, ALOUD offers to open the site in its own window so you can sign in once; after that its articles load in full. ALOUD doesn't get around paywalls you don't subscribe to.

Library

The library (book icon, top left) shows your documents with covers, how much is left to listen to, and a search box. Hover a card for + Up next and × (removes it from the library; the file itself is not touched).

  • Continue listening at the top shows what you're in the middle of, with a ring for how far you are; click to carry on where you left off.
  • Folders: New folder, then drag documents onto it. Folders can hold folders. Right-click a folder for Colour (like Finder tags) and Emoji. Hover a folder to fan out what's inside.
  • Peek: hover a document and press Space.

Voices, speed and sound

Choose a voice in settings (⌘,). ▶︎ plays a sample. English voices include Heart, Bella, Michael, Emma and George. ALOUD picks a voice that matches the document's language when one is available.

Clean up voice removes the boxy “in a can” colour. Speed goes from 0.6× to 3× with − +.

Reading feel

  • Focus mode (F, or the frame button at the top): while playing, everything but the passage being read fades back, in the text and on the PDF. The player bar hides while focus mode is on (Space still plays and pauses); press F again to bring it back. Move the mouse to see the whole page for a moment.
  • Smooth highlight: the highlight fills each word as it's said.
  • Ambient colour: the page takes a soft tint from the document's cover; the library's colours follow the time of day.
  • Progress: a thin line along the top of the window, a ring on the Dock icon, ticks on the progress bar for each section (hover for its name) and a small waveform of the passage being read.
  • Sounds: very quiet clicks for play, pause, marks and notes.
  • Panels and cards can be flicked down to close them.

Each can be turned off in settings (⌘,) or from ⌘K.

More

  • Sleep timer (⏾ in the player): click to cycle through 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or the end of the current section.
  • Save as audio (⤓ at the top): one audio file per section in your Downloads folder, for your phone or a walk.
  • Themes (settings): Light, Sepia, Dark, or Auto to follow your Mac.

Privacy

Text, voices, OCR and math recognition all run on this Mac. Nothing you read is uploaded. The only network use is fetching a link you paste, and the links you click on a citation card.

Troubleshooting

  • Something is read that shouldn't be, or skipped wrongly: turn on Show what isn't read to see exactly what's skipped, and note the page.
  • A word sounds wrong: right-click it › pronunciation.
  • A scanned PDF opens slowly the first time: its text is being recognised; it's instant afterwards.
  • ⌃⌥R doesn't work: see “Read anything on your Mac” above.
  • The browser button says “Open ALOUD first”: ALOUD must be running.
